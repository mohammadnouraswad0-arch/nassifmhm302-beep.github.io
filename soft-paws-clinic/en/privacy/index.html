Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Soft Paws Veterinary Clinic is committed to protecting the privacy of pet owners and their animals.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only essential data required for appointments or store orders: name, phone number, optional email, and basic pet clinical details.

2. Secure Access

Appointments and orders cannot be accessed with a reference code alone; phone number verification is mandatory.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Records are retained solely for veterinary continuity of care. You may request deletion via t.azakeer@softpaws.clinic.
